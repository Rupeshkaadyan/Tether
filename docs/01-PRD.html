01Executive summary

Tether is a private, two-person relationship app. It helps a couple build a small daily practice together — one prompt, one mood, one shared reflection — while respecting that the two people involved may not share the same faith, worldview, or cultural background.

Every major competitor (Paired, Lasting, Couply, Evergreen, Relish, Twogle) is either secular-generic or implicitly Christian. None of them let a Hindu–Muslim, Christian–atheist, or interfaith couple see themselves in the product. In the United States roughly four in ten marriages cross religious lines and about thirty percent of adults are religiously unaffiliated. That is the market Tether serves, and it is currently unserved.

Positioning statement

Tether is the relationship app for couples who don't share a single worldview. Each partner chooses their own wisdom track. The practice is shared; the beliefs are not.

03Goals and non-goals

Goals

  • G1 — Multi-worldview couples feel genuinely represented.
  • G2 — Pairing completion above 60%.
  • G3 — Day-30 retention above 25%.
  • G4 — Deliver full value when only one partner joins.
  • G5 — Be the safest, most private app in the category.
  • G6 — Reach $10k MRR within 12 months of US launch.

Non-goals (explicitly out of scope)

  • Not therapy, and not a replacement for it.
  • Not a dating or matchmaking product.
  • Not a social network in v1.
  • Not a crisis or emergency service — we detect and refer.
  • Not a co-parenting, household logistics, or finance tool.
  • No advertising, ever. No selling of user content.

04Target users and personas

P1 — Priya & Alex Primary

Interfaith couple, US, married 2 years. Priya is Hindu, Alex is lapsed Catholic. Both 29, urban, dual-income. They argue about holidays and about how to raise future children. Priya wants structure; Alex is sceptical of "apps for feelings." Neither has found a relationship product that reflects them.

  • Goal — Build a shared practice without either person converting or compromising.
  • Frustration — Every app either pushes Christian framing or strips all meaning out.
  • Tether wins when — Each sees their own wisdom in the app, and shared prompts feel neutral but not empty.

P2 — Maya & Jordan Primary

Secular, therapy-literate, US, together 5 years. Both in individual therapy. They know Gottman and attachment theory. They want evidence-based tools, not inspirational quotes.

  • Goal — Practical skill-building: conflict repair, emotional bids, repair attempts.
  • Frustration — Finds competitor content shallow and repetitive.
  • Tether wins when — Content is clearly grounded in Gottman, EFT, attachment, and NVC — and says so.

P3 — Fatima & Dan Secondary

Fatima is Muslim, Dan is agnostic. Navigating family expectations and in-law dynamics. Dan worries about being asked to believe something; Fatima worries about being asked to let go of something.

P4 — Solo Sarah Critical

Sarah wants to work on her relationship. Her partner has downloaded three couples apps and opened none of them. Every app she has tried has dead-ended at the invite screen.

  • Goal — Start anyway, without waiting for permission or participation.
  • Frustration — "Come back when your partner joins" is a dead end.
  • Tether wins when — She gets real value on day one alone, and everything merges when he eventually joins.

P5 — Long-distance Later

Different time zones, asynchronous. Needs time-zone-aware prompt delivery and no "you missed yesterday" guilt.

07Functional requirements

Priority key: Must v0.1 blocker   Should v1.0   Could later

7.1 Identity and onboarding

IDRequirementPriAcceptance criteria
FR-001Account creationMustSign in with Apple, Google, or email. No phone number required — competitors lose users here. Account created in under 20 seconds.
FR-002Per-partner wisdom trackMustEach partner independently selects a track: Secular/Psychology, Vedic/Dharmic, Quranic, Biblical. At v0.1 ship Secular + Biblical. Choice is per user, not per couple, and is editable in settings.
FR-003Love Languages assessmentMustStandard 5-language assessment, ~20 questions, under 2 minutes. Result shown immediately. Stored per user. Framed as a fun tool, never as science — see FR-041.
FR-0047-day onboarding ritualMustDay 1 track · Day 2 Love Languages · Day 3 first prompt · Day 4 invite partner · Day 5 first shared entry · Day 6 Pulse baseline · Day 7 AI coach intro. Each step skippable.
FR-005Notification permissionMustRequested after first value moment (day 3), not at launch. Pre-permission explainer screen shown first.

7.2 Pairing — the critical path

IDRequirementPriAcceptance criteria
FR-010Three invite pathsMustEvery invite offers: shareable deep link, QR code, and 6-digit code. All three land the partner in the app on the correct screen, authed or not.
FR-011Waiting roomMustNever a dead spinner. Shows invite status, time sent, and a copyable pre-written message to send. Manual "check again" control.
FR-012Nudge sequenceMustAt 24h and 72h, one-tap re-invite via iMessage, WhatsApp, or SMS. Max 3 nudges, then stop.
FR-013In-person QR pairingMustScanning a partner's QR while both are present pairs instantly, no network round-trip dependency. Eliminates the entire class of remote link failures.
FR-01472-hour Solo Mode fallbackMustIf no pairing after 72h, proactively offer: "Start solo. Everything syncs the moment they join." One tap. This is the single most important screen in the product.
FR-015Pairing confirmationMustBoth partners receive a push. A celebration moment, not a toast. Marks the start of the shared streak.
FR-016Solo → paired mergeMustAll pre-pairing content is preserved and unlocked together in a merge ceremony. Nothing is lost, nothing is retroactively hidden.
FR-017UnpairShouldEither partner can unpair. Requires confirmation. Content is retained per user, not deleted.

7.3 The daily loop

IDRequirementPriAcceptance criteria
FR-020One daily promptMustExactly one prompt per day per couple. Not four. Derived from the intersection of both partners' tracks.
FR-021Mood tapMustSingle 1–5 selection. One tap. No text required.
FR-022One-sentence replyMustSingle-line input, 280 characters. Voice-to-text enabled and encouraged.
FR-023Hidden until both replyMustA reply is invisible to the other partner until both have answered. Drives reciprocity and removes performance pressure.
FR-024Streak with freezeMustCounts consecutive days both replied. Two automatic freeze days per month. Never punitive language.
FR-025Smart send timeShouldLearns each partner's active hours and sends individually. Never both partners at the same timestamp.
FR-026Solo mode loopMustFull loop works for one user: prompts, mood, journal, Pulse, AI coach. No partner-gated surfaces.

7.4 Journal, Pulse, and insight

IDRequirementPriAcceptance criteria
FR-030Couple journalMustMood + one line. Optional private-to-me toggle on any entry. Chronological, searchable, exportable.
FR-031Relationship PulseMustThree states: Thriving, Drifting, Strained. Computed from mood, reply cadence, and sentiment. Tap through shows why.
FR-032Weekly recapShouldSunday summary: streak, Pulse trend, a highlighted memory, one suggested focus. Push + in-app.
FR-033Trend awarenessShouldSurfaces sustained change: "You've been drifting for two weeks." Gentle, never alarmist.

7.5 AI coach

IDRequirementPriAcceptance criteria
FR-040Conversational coachMustFree-text and voice input. Responds with grounded, non-generic guidance. Latency under 3s to first token.
FR-041Evidence groundingMustCore guidance draws on Gottman Method, EFT, attachment theory, NVC. Love Languages is used for engagement only — the model has limited empirical support and must never be presented as clinical.
FR-042Per-partner wisdom groundingMustResponses reflect the asking partner's own track, not a generic blend.
FR-043Couple memoryShouldRetrieves relevant past entries and prior conversations. Can reference a conflict from three weeks ago. Session context persists.
FR-044Voice modeShouldFull voice input and spoken response. Nobody types during a real disagreement.
FR-045De-escalation modeCouldWhen both partners log high-distress mood within a short window, proactively offer a cool-down protocol.
FR-046Scope refusalMustRefuses diagnosis, medication advice, legal advice, and divorce recommendation. Redirects to human professionals.

7.6 Safety, privacy, and trust

IDRequirementPriAcceptance criteria
FR-050Safety classifierMustAll AI input and all journal text passes a crisis classifier before storage or response. Detects self-harm, suicidality, abuse, and violence.
FR-051Crisis escalationMustOn detection: suppress all couple-facing output, show a partner-only resource card with US hotlines (988, National Domestic Violence Hotline). Never surface one partner's crisis signal to the other.
FR-052Pause stateShouldUser can pause all prompts and notifications for 1–30 days without losing streak or data.
FR-053End-to-end encryptionMustJournal entries and AI conversations encrypted client-side. Server stores ciphertext only and cannot read content.
FR-054Data export and deleteMustOne-tap full export (JSON + human-readable). One-tap full account deletion, hard delete within 30 days.
FR-055No content analyticsMustBehavioural analytics only. Journal and chat content is never sent to any analytics provider. Stated publicly.
FR-056Scope disclaimerMustShown at onboarding, in ToS, and persistently near the AI coach: Tether is not therapy and not a crisis service.

7.7 Monetisation

IDRequirementPriAcceptance criteria
FR-060One subscription covers bothMustIndustry standard. Either partner purchasing unlocks premium for both. Stated on the paywall, pricing screen, and App Store subtitle.
FR-061Free tierMustFree forever: Solo Mode, 1 prompt/week, mood tracking, first-track content, Love Languages, 5 AI messages/month.
FR-06214-day trialMustFull premium, no ads, covers both partners. Longer than the category-standard 7 days.
FR-063PricingMustUS $12.99/mo, $79.99/yr, $149.99/yr Couples+. India ₹149/mo, ₹899/yr, ₹1,799/yr Couples+. PPP tiers handled by store.

08User flows

8.1 Onboarding

  1. Welcome — name, and the promise: two people, one practice.
  2. Wisdom track — four options, each partner chooses their own. "You can change this later."
  3. Love Languages — 20 questions, under 2 minutes, immediate result.
  4. First prompt — answered solo. Delivers value before asking for anything.
  5. Notification permission — with pre-explainer, after value is proven.
  6. Invite partner — three paths offered simultaneously.
  7. Waiting room or continue solo — never a dead end.

8.2 Pairing

  1. Invite screen — link, QR, and code all visible at once.
  2. Waiting room — live status, copyable message, manual refresh.
  3. 24h — gentle nudge option.
  4. 72h — proactive Solo Mode offer: "Start now. Everything syncs when they join."
  5. Partner joins — merge ceremony, both notified, shared streak begins.

8.3 Daily loop

  1. Push — personalised send time, per partner.
  2. Open — one prompt, immediately visible. No navigation required.
  3. Mood tap — one tap.
  4. Reply — one sentence, voice or text.
  5. Lock — hidden until partner replies.
  6. Unlock — both see both. Optional reaction.
  7. Streak — updated, with freeze days available.

8.4 Safety escalation

  1. Input — user writes in journal or AI chat.
  2. Classify — on-device pre-screen, then server classifier before storage.
  3. Trigger — crisis signal detected.
  4. Suppress — no couple-facing output. Partner sees nothing.
  5. Support — resource card, only to the user who wrote it.
  6. Log — safety event recorded for review, content not stored in plaintext.

09User stories and acceptance criteria

Epic A — I can see myself in this product

  • As an interfaith partner, I want to choose my own wisdom track so that I'm not force-fed someone else's beliefs. Accept: track selected independently, reflected in all my content, changeable in settings.
  • As a secular user, I want evidence-based content so I trust the guidance. Accept: methodology cited, no faith framing on the Secular track.

Epic B — I can start, even alone

  • As the more enthusiastic partner, I want to begin without waiting so the app isn't useless to me. Accept: Solo Mode fully functional on day one; no partner-gated surfaces.
  • As a solo user whose partner later joins, I want my history preserved. Accept: merge ceremony, zero data loss.

Epic C — We can actually pair

  • As an inviter, I want multiple ways to invite so a broken link doesn't kill it. Accept: link, QR, and code all functional; in-person QR always works.
  • As someone whose partner hasn't responded, I want to know what to do. Accept: waiting room shows status and offers a copyable message; 72h solo fallback.

Epic D — The habit sticks

  • As a busy partner, I want the daily action to be tiny. Accept: prompt + mood + one sentence completable in under 60 seconds.
  • As someone who missed a day, I want grace not guilt. Accept: two freeze days per month, no punitive copy.

Epic E — I am safe here

  • As a user in crisis, I want appropriate help. Accept: hotlines surfaced immediately, partner never notified.
  • As a privacy-conscious user, I want my words unreadable to the company. Accept: E2E encryption on journal and chat; clearly stated.
  • As a paying user, I want to cancel easily. Accept: under 3 taps, in-app or web, no dark patterns.

10Content requirements

Content architecture — the real moat

Layer 1 Universal psychological core (Gottman, EFT, attachment, NVC)  →  Layer 2 Wisdom track expression (Secular, Vedic, Quranic, Biblical)  →  Layer 3 Cultural context (US individualist, US immigrant, India joint-family, Global)  →  Layer 4 Language.

One core, many expressions. This is how you scale to eight languages without eight times the writing.

  • Volume at v0.1 — 90 days of daily prompts, minimum. Two tracks live (Secular, Biblical). US cultural context only.
  • Quality bar — benchmark against the Gottman Card Decks, which offer 1,000+ research-backed prompts for free. Any prompt that reads like a horoscope gets cut.
  • Review gate — every faith-track prompt reviewed by a practitioner of that tradition. Non-negotiable for credibility.
  • Tone — warm, plain, non-clinical. Never cheerful when the content is serious. No exclamation marks in sensitive contexts.
  • Never — diagnose, prescribe, shame, or assume the relationship should continue.

12Risks, dependencies, open questions

Partner never joinsHighSolo Mode and pairing suite are both in v0.1. This is the primary bet.

Open questions

  • Free tier AI message cap — 5/month, or unlimited with a weaker model?
  • Should Relationship Pulse ever predict, or only describe? Recommendation: describe only, in v1.
  • Anonymous mode — can a user try Tether without an account? Recommendation: yes, deferred to v1.0.
  • Faith-track reviewer compensation model — volunteer advisory board or paid?

13Launch criteria

Definition of done for v0.1
  • All Must requirements shipped and tested.
  • 90 days of prompts live for both launch tracks, faith-reviewed.
  • Safety classifier tested against a red-team set with zero misses.
  • E2E encryption independently verified — server cannot read content.
  • Pairing funnel instrumented end to end.
  • 50 real couples through TestFlight, minimum 2 weeks.
  • App Store review requirements met, including account deletion.
  • Legal: ToS, privacy policy, disclaimers, LLC formed.

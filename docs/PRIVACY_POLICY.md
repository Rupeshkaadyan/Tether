# Tether Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 18 September 2026

Tether is built on one promise: **your words stay yours.** This policy explains,
in plain language, what happens to your data. The short version is that almost
nothing leaves your device, and we have designed the app so that we could not
read your journal even if we wanted to.

---

## 1. Where your data lives

Everything you write — journal entries, mood logs, prompt replies, and
conversations with the Coach — is stored **on your device** using Apple's
SwiftData. There is no Tether server holding your journal, and there is no
account to sign in to.

## 2. Encryption

Your entries, Coach conversations, and memories are sealed with an encryption
key that is generated on your device and stored in the device Keychain. It
never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us.

**The practical consequence:** we cannot read your words, and we cannot recover
them for you. If you lose every device and have not enabled iCloud sync, there
is no way to get your journal back. That is the cost of us not being able to
read it.

## 3. What we collect

**Nothing.** Tether has:

- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising SDKs
- No third-party data brokers
- No crash-reporting that carries your content
- No user accounts, email addresses, or phone numbers

You can use every part of Tether without telling us who you are.

## 4. Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud, your entries are mirrored to **your own private iCloud
database** via Apple's CloudKit. This syncs your data across your own devices.
It is your Apple ID and your storage — we have no access to it. Data is sealed
on your device *before* it is stored, so what reaches iCloud is already
encrypted.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings without losing your local data.

## 5. Weekly reflection

Your weekly reflection is written **on your device**. It is computed from your
own entries and never sent anywhere.

If you explicitly turn on **Deeper reflections** in Settings (it is **off by
default**), the app may send an anonymized summary to our server to generate a
richer reflection. That summary contains **only**:

- how many times you checked in,
- your average mood and how it changed,
- recurring themes as single words,
- your streak length,
- your chosen wisdom track.

It **never** contains your actual words, entry text, names, or any identifier.
Turning the setting off immediately stops this.

## 6. Other device features

- **Reminders** — scheduled entirely on-device. We never see whether you
  enabled them.
- **Voice input** — uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is
  transcribed on your device and is not uploaded.
- **Camera / Photos** — Tether does not request access to either.

## 7. Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, payment is handled by Apple (and, in future,
RevenueCat as Apple's authorised reseller). **We never see or store your card
details.** We receive only a receipt token that tells us whether a subscription
is active.

## 8. Deleting your data

**Settings → Delete all my data** permanently erases everything on this device.
Deleting the app does the same. If you enabled iCloud, also remove the
Tether data from your iCloud account to complete deletion.

## 9. Children

Tether is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we
do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 10. Tether is not a crisis service

Tether is a relationship reflection tool. It is **not therapy, not medical
advice, and not an emergency service.** If you are in danger, please contact
your local emergency number or a crisis line.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data leaves your device, we will update this policy and
tell you in the app before the change takes effect. We will never quietly start
reading or uploading your journal.

# Tether — App Description Prompt

A copy-paste brief describing what Tether is, what it does, and what it looks
like. Use the whole thing, or lift just the section you need.

---

## THE PROMPT

> **Tether** is a private iOS app for two people in a relationship. Each partner
> answers **one short question a day** — one sentence is enough — and neither sees
> the other's answer until both have replied. It is a small daily ritual, not a
> curriculum and not therapy.
>
> Its defining idea: **each partner chooses their own wisdom track** — Secular &
> Psychology, Biblical, Vedic & Dharmic, or Quranic. A Hindu and a Christian
> partner each get content in their own framework while sharing the same daily
> practice. Every other couples app assumes a shared worldview; Tether is built
> for when there isn't one.
>
> **Tone:** warm, calm, plain-spoken. Never clinical, never preachy, never
> cheerful about something serious. Quiet confidence rather than excitement.

## WHAT IT CAN DO

**Onboarding (4 steps)**
Name → choose your wisdom track → a 20-question Love Languages assessment →
answer your first prompt. Under two minutes.

**The daily loop — the core of the app**
One prompt. A five-point mood tap. One sentence, typed or dictated. The answer
stays **hidden until both partners reply**, so nobody performs. A streak counts
consecutive days, with two automatic freeze days a month and no guilt when you
miss.

**Solo mode — the thing competitors get wrong**
Every feature works with one person. A partner who never installs the app does
not lock you out. When they eventually join, everything merges in a celebration
and nothing is lost.

**Pairing that actually completes**
Three ways to connect — a shareable link, a QR code you scan together in person,
or a 6-digit code. A waiting room that shows real status and a ready-to-send
message instead of a dead spinner. Gentle nudges capped at three. After 72 hours
of silence, the app stops waiting and hands you the product: *"Start on your own.
Everything syncs the moment they join."*

**Relationship Pulse**
A read on the last two weeks built from three things: how you have been feeling,
how often you showed up, and how steady it has been. Four states — Thriving,
Drifting, Strained, or not enough data yet. It **describes, it never predicts.**

**Weekly recap**
Sunday summary: streak, entries, average mood, one memory worth keeping, and a
single suggested focus for the coming week.

**An AI coach with memory**
Ask it anything. It remembers what you have written — it can reference a
conversation from three weeks ago — and it speaks from *your* wisdom track, not
a generic blend. It names the specific thing rather than offering a platitude.

**Safety, built in from the start**
Everything you type is screened. If the app detects a crisis — self-harm, abuse,
coercive control — it **stops generating a normal reply** and shows confidential
helplines instead. Your partner is never told. Entries like that are never
remembered, so they cannot resurface later.

**Private by architecture**
Journal entries, coach conversations, and memories are sealed with AES-256-GCM
using a key that lives only in the device's Keychain. The company cannot read
them. Settings says the honest cost out loud: *lose every device and the
encrypted history is gone — that is the price of us not being able to read it.*

**Pricing**
Free tier, then a 3-day trial. One subscription covers **both partners**.
Yearly $79.99, monthly $12.99, or Couples+ at $149.99 with two live coach
sessions.

**Also included**
Four wisdom tracks with 90 days of prompts each (360 total) · a journal with
per-entry private or shared control · reminders timed to each partner
individually · settings to change track, pause prompts, export everything, or
delete all data permanently.

## WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE

**The feeling:** warm paper, not clinical white. Calm and unhurried. Soft shapes
and generous space. Something you would not mind a partner seeing over your
shoulder.

**The mark.** A single curve connecting two points, with visible slack between
them — two people, joined, still free to move. It appears as the app icon, as a
large hero on the welcome screen, as a faint backdrop behind content, and as the
animation when two partners connect. One shape, used everywhere.

**Colour**

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#FDFBF8` warm cream | Every screen. Never pure white. |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards floating on the cream |
| Text | `#2A2438` warm near-black | Headings and body |
| Muted | `#6F6879` | Secondary text |
| Brand | `#5B4BC4` indigo | Primary actions |
| Brand gradient | `#6A57D6` → `#4A3AA8` | Buttons, the daily prompt card |
| Warm | `#E08A4B` terracotta | The emotional counterweight |
| Rose | `#D96A8A` | Accents, celebration |
| Thriving | `#2E9E6B` green | Positive pulse |
| Drifting | `#D08A28` amber | Caution |
| Strained | `#C4463F` red | Needs attention |

**Track identity colours** — each tradition has its own, so you can tell at a
glance whose content you are looking at:
Secular `#4A7DBF` blue · Biblical `#7B6BD6` violet · Vedic `#D98A2B` saffron ·
Quranic `#2E9E7B` emerald

**Type.** SF Pro Rounded throughout — soft and human rather than editorial.
Display 34pt bold, screen titles 28pt, the daily prompt 21pt semibold in white on
the gradient, body 16.5pt, captions 13pt.

**Shape and depth.** Cards are white with 24pt rounded corners, a hairline warm
border, and a very soft shadow — lifted off the page but never heavy. Buttons are
54pt tall with the brand gradient and a gentle spring when pressed.

**Motion.** Slow and calm. A 4.5-second breathing gradient sits behind the daily
prompt so there is a moment to breathe before answering. Screens cross-fade at
250ms. When partners connect, two avatars glide together. Nothing bounces,
nothing celebrates loudly.

**The screens**

1. **Welcome** — a 400pt dusk-gradient panel with the mark and the wordmark
   "Two people. One practice.", rounded at the bottom, name field below.
2. **Wisdom track** — four cards, each with a coloured icon disc, equal weight,
   no ranking. *"This is yours alone. Your partner chooses their own."*
3. **Home** — a time-aware greeting, your name large, a streak ring, and two
   avatars (the partner's a dashed circle until they join). Below: the Pulse
   card, then the gradient prompt card as the hero, then mood dots and one input.
4. **Waiting room** — a live QR code, a copyable 6-digit code, and a pre-written
   message: *"Not sure what to say? Send this."*
5. **Coach** — soft chat bubbles, your messages in indigo, the coach's on cream.
   A small "Remembering" label appears when it draws on your past.
6. **Crisis card** — calm, high contrast, unbranded. Helplines and one line:
   *"Your partner is not told about this."*

## ONE-LINE VERSIONS

- **App Store subtitle:** Two people. One practice.
- **Short pitch:** The relationship app for couples who don't share a single
  worldview. Each partner chooses their own wisdom track; the practice is shared.
- **For a designer:** Warm, calm, paper-feeling iOS app. Cream background, indigo
  gradient, one curve motif used everywhere. Soft rounded shapes, generous space,
  slow motion. Should feel like a quiet ritual, not a productivity tool.
- **For an investor:** The only relationship app serving interfaith and
  mixed-belief couples — roughly four in ten US marriages — through per-partner
  wisdom tracks. Local-first, end-to-end encrypted, with an AI coach that
  remembers the relationship.

01Design principles

1. Quiet, not loud

People open this app at vulnerable moments. No confetti explosions, no streak-shaming, no bright red badges. Celebration is a warm shift in colour, not a party.

2. One thing per screen

The daily prompt screen contains the prompt and nothing else. If a user has to look for the primary action, the screen has failed.

3. Never a dead end

No screen may exist where the user cannot proceed. Waiting, empty, or failed states always carry a next action. This principle directly targets the failure mode that damaged the predecessor product.

4. Both people, always visible

Where content is shared, both partners' presence is visible — avatars, names, states. Tether is not a single-player app wearing a couples costume.

Colour rules

Track accents identify content, never decorate it. Never colour-code partners by gender. All semantic states must be distinguishable in greyscale — colour is never the only signal.

Molecules

  • PromptCard — the day's question, date, and a track chip. The hero of the home screen.
  • ReplyRow — avatar, one-line reply, timestamp. Locked state shows a covered placeholder with "Reveals when you both answer."
  • PartnerRow — avatar, name, last-active, nudge action. Used in waiting room and settings.
  • InviteMethod — link, QR, code. All three always visible together, never behind a tab.
  • WisdomTrackCard — icon, name, one-line description, selected state. Four cards, equal visual weight — no implied ranking.
  • JournalEntry — mood dot, text, visibility indicator, date.
  • CoachBubble — AI message with streaming indicator and a persistent "not therapy" affordance.
  • ResourceCard — crisis resources. Calm, high contrast, no branding flourish.
  • PaywallFeature — icon, title, one-line benefit. Privacy promise appears as a feature row.

Organisms

  • DailyLoop — PromptCard + MoodDot row + input + lock state + streak. The entire habit in one component.
  • InviteSheet — three methods, share sheet integration, copy-to-clipboard.
  • WaitingRoom — status, elapsed time, copyable message, refresh, solo escape hatch.
  • CoachThread — message list, voice button, streaming, disclaimer footer.
  • PulsePanel — current state, trend, tap-through explanation.

05Motion specification

InteractionDurationEasing / notes
Screen transition250msStandard ease-out. Cross-dissolve for tab changes.
Reply unlock reveal400msThe one moment earned theatre. Gentle scale and fade, never confetti.
Mood dot select150msImmediate tactile feedback.
AI token streaming—Typewriter, 18ms/token. Never a blocking spinner.
Pairing success600msWarm brand-tint wash and both avatars converging. Celebration without noise.
Loading—Skeleton screens, never spinners, for anything over 400ms.

Reduced motion — honour the OS setting. Replace all animation with instant state change or opacity-only fades. No exceptions.

06Accessibility

  • WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Contrast 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components.
  • Dynamic Type — all text scales to 200% without clipping or overlap. Test every screen at maximum.
  • VoiceOver / TalkBack — every interactive element labelled. Mood dots announce state and value ("3 of 5").
  • Touch targets — 44×44pt minimum. Mood dots are 36pt visually but must have a 44pt hit area.
  • Colour independence — no state communicated by colour alone. Pulse, mood, and streak all carry text or shape.
  • Reduced motion and increased contrast both honoured.

08Screen specifications

Screen 3 — Wisdom track selection P0

AspectSpecification
PurposeUser selects the framework their content will speak from.
Header"What wisdom speaks to you?" Subhead: "This is yours alone. Your partner chooses their own."
ContentFour cards, equal size and visual weight: Secular & Psychology · Vedic & Dharmic · Quranic · Biblical. Each with icon, name, and one-line description. At v0.1 ship Secular and Biblical live; others show "Coming soon" rather than being hidden — the promise matters.
InteractionTap to select, single choice. Continue button disabled until chosen.
StatesSelected (brand border, 2pt, accent leading bar). Unselected (1pt neutral border). Coming soon (reduced opacity, badge).
Critical copy"Yours alone" must appear. It is the entire differentiator and must be legible, not buried.

Screen 5 — Home / daily loop P0

AspectSpecification
PurposeThe whole habit. Completable in under 60 seconds.
Layout (top→bottom)Date and streak ring · PromptCard with today's question · MoodDot row (5 dots) · one-line input with voice button · partner status row showing locked or revealed state.
Locked statePartner's reply area shows a covered placeholder: "Reveals when you both answer." Never show a partial or blurred reply — that reads as a tease and erodes trust.
Revealed stateBoth replies visible, side by side or stacked with avatars. Optional reaction. No comment thread in v1.
Solo variantNo lock. Reply saves immediately. A quiet, non-nagging invite affordance below.
Missed dayOffer freeze rather than showing a broken streak. Copy: "Keep your streak? You have 2 freezes left."
Paywall hookFree tier sees a limited number of prompts per week. Never block today's prompt if the user has already started it.

Screen 6 & 7 — Invite and waiting room P0

This is the most important flow in the product

The predecessor product failed here. Every screen must offer a way forward. No state in this flow may be terminal.

AspectSpecification
Invite screenAll three methods visible simultaneously: a large share button, a QR code, and a 6-digit code. No tabs, no hiding.
Waiting roomShows: elapsed time since invite, a pre-written copyable message ("Hey — I found something I'd like us to try together"), a manual refresh, and nudge controls.
NeverAn unexplained spinner. A blank state. A screen with no action available.
24h"Invite sent yesterday. Send a reminder?" with one-tap WhatsApp / iMessage / SMS.
72hProactive offer, visually prominent: "Start on your own. Everything you write syncs the moment they join." One tap into Solo Mode.
SuccessBoth avatars converge, brand-tint wash, both partners pushed. Marks shared streak start.

Screen 12 — AI coach P0

AspectSpecification
LayoutMessage thread, coach avatar top-left, input bar with text and voice. Persistent footer: "Tether is not therapy."
StreamingTypewriter from 18ms per token. Typing indicator before first token. Never a blocking spinner.
VoicePress-and-hold or tap mic. Waveform while recording. Spoken playback option on responses.
MemoryWhen the coach references past context, show a subtle "Remembering" indicator. Transparency builds trust and differentiates from a stateless chatbot.
RefusalWarm and brief, never a lecture. Offer the next best step (a human professional, a prompt, or a pause).
PrivacyHeader affordance opens encryption explanation. "Your conversations are encrypted. We cannot read them."

Screen 13 — Paywall P0

  • Headline — a benefit, not a feature list.
  • Critical line, prominent — "One subscription. Both partners. Always."
  • Feature rows — daily prompts, full content library, AI coach, unlimited journal. Include privacy as a feature row ("End-to-end encrypted").
  • Options — annual ($79.99) pre-selected with savings shown, monthly ($12.99) secondary.
  • Trial — "14 days free, then $79.99/year. Cancel anytime."
  • Placement — after the first value moment, never on launch.
  • Restore — visible text link. Required.

09States

StateRequirements
EmptyMust teach, not just fill space. Journal empty state shows a sample entry with "Your entries will look like this." Always carries a primary action.
LoadingSkeleton screens above 400ms. Spinners only for sub-400ms operations.
ErrorPlain language, what happened, what to do. Never an error code alone. Always a retry.
OfflineQuiet persistent banner: "Offline — your entries are saved." Never a blocking modal. Full functionality retained.
PausedIf the user has paused notifications, show a gentle "Resume" affordance, not a warning.
CrisisCalm, high contrast, unbranded. Resource card replaces normal content. Partner sees nothing.

10Voice and tone

We are

Warm · plain · calm · respectful · brief

We are not

Cheerful · clinical · preachy · cutesy · urgent

We never

Guilt · diagnose · prescribe · assume · shame

Write thisNot this
"Start on your own. Everything syncs when they join.""Come back when your partner joins!"
"Keep your streak? You have 2 freezes left.""You broke your 12-day streak!"
"You've been drifting for two weeks.""Your relationship is in trouble."
"This is yours alone. Your partner chooses their own.""Select your faith."
"Tether isn't therapy. Here's what might help.""I'm not able to help with that."

Sensitive content rules — no exclamation marks anywhere near conflict, crisis, or Pulse. Sentence case everywhere, never title case for buttons. No emoji in system copy.

11Localisation readiness

  • Build RTL-ready now, even though v0.1 is English-only. Retrofitting directionality is expensive; Urdu and Arabic are on the roadmap.
  • Text expansion — allow 35% growth. German and Hindi both run longer than English.
  • No text in images. Ever. All strings externalised from day one.
  • Date, number, and name formatting — locale-aware via Intl APIs.
  • Launch sequence — English (v0.1) → Spanish (v1.0) → Hindi (v1.5) → Tamil, Telugu, Urdu (v2.0). A language ships only when a native speaker owns its content.

## Description (4000 max)

```
Tether is a private daily ritual for two people.

One question a day. One line each. When you have both answered, you see
each other's answer and can reply in a sentence. That is the whole idea.

It takes under a minute. That is the point.

────────────

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

Most couples apps want your data. Tether does not want it, cannot read it,
and never asks for an account.

• No sign-up. No email. No phone number.
• Your entries are sealed with a key that lives only in your iPhone's
  Keychain. We cannot read them.
• Nothing is sent to analytics. Ever.
• It works completely offline.
• Your partner never sees anything you mark private.

────────────

WHAT YOU GET

• A daily question, written to be answered honestly in one sentence
• Your private journal, as a timeline you will actually want to scroll
• A gentle reveal when you have both answered
• A one-line reply, so the conversation continues
• A care nudge when they have had a hard day
• Relationship Pulse — a plain read on how things have been going
• Insights drawn from your own writing, not a template
• Weekly reflection, milestones, and a 7-day challenge
• Face ID lock, so nobody else can open it
• Light and dark mode
• English, हिन्दी and Español

────────────

FOUR WISDOM PATHS

Tether does not assume what you believe.

Choose Secular & Psychology, Biblical, Vedic & Dharmic, or Quranic — and
each partner chooses their own. An interfaith couple can each practise in
the tradition that is theirs. The relationship is shared; the worldview is
personal.

────────────

WHAT IT IS NOT

Tether is not therapy and not a crisis service. It is not a dating app, not
a social network, and not a game. There are no leaderboards, no ads, and no
streak-shaming. If you miss a day, the app says nothing about it.

A safety check runs on everything you type. If it detects a crisis, the app
stops and shows you support lines instead. Your partner is never told.

────────────

PRICING

One subscription covers both partners. Always. Free to start — the daily
ritual, your journal and the reveal are yours from day one.

────────────

MADE FOR TWO PEOPLE WHO WANT TO KEEP SHOWING UP

If you have ever finished a long day and realised you barely spoke, this is
for you. One minute. One question. One line back.

Different paths. A deeper connection.
```

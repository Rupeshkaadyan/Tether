# Biblical Track — 90 Daily Prompts

For theological and pastoral review. Days 1-90.

The arc is deliberate. Conflict work does not begin until day 43; two weeks
of gratitude and attention come first.

## Arc 1 - Noticing (days 1-14)

**01.** Where did you notice grace this week — something good you did not earn?  
*category: grace*

**02.** What is one thing your partner did today that you have not thanked them for?  
*category: gratitude*

**03.** Where did you see God at work in your home this week?  
*category: presence*

**04.** What is something about them you have stopped noticing because it is always there?  
*category: noticing*

**05.** What did they do recently that made your day lighter?  
*category: gratitude*

**06.** When did you last laugh together? What set it off?  
*category: joy*

**07.** What ordinary thing they do would you miss if it stopped?  
*category: gratitude*

**08.** Who did they serve this week — you, or someone else? How did that land?  
*category: service*

**09.** What is something they are better at than you, and you are thankful for it?  
*category: gratitude*

**10.** When did you feel proud of them this week?  
*category: gratitude*

**11.** What is one thing they said recently that stayed with you?  
*category: noticing*

**12.** Where did you see them try this week, even if it did not land?  
*category: repair*

**13.** What are you looking forward to about them today?  
*category: hope*

**14.** Name one thing you thank God for in them that you have never said aloud.  
*category: blessing*

## Arc 2 - Knowing each other (days 15-28)

**15.** When you are stressed, do you want space or company? Does that match what you usually get?  
*category: needs*

**16.** When you are hurting, what helps — and what makes it worse?  
*category: needs*

**17.** What did you learn about love from the house you grew up in?  
*category: history*

**18.** What is something you are afraid to need?  
*category: vulnerability*

**19.** How do you know when you are at your limit?  
*category: self-awareness*

**20.** What does feeling safe actually feel like for you?  
*category: safety*

**21.** What is a need you have quietly stopped asking for?  
*category: needs*

**22.** When did you last feel truly known by them?  
*category: connection*

**23.** What is something about your day you never get to tell them?  
*category: disclosure*

**24.** What drains you that they might not realise?  
*category: self-awareness*

**25.** What restores you that they might not realise?  
*category: self-awareness*

**26.** What small ritual makes you feel close to them?  
*category: rituals*

**27.** When do you feel most like yourself around them?  
*category: identity*

**28.** What is one thing you wish they asked you about more often?  
*category: needs*

## Arc 3 - Being heard (days 29-42)

*category: expression*

**30.** When did you reach for them this week and it went unnoticed?  
*category: bids*

**31.** What is a conversation the two of you keep postponing?  
*category: avoidance*

**32.** How do you want hard things told to you?  
*category: expression*

**33.** Scripture says be quick to listen and slow to speak. Where did you get that backwards this week?  
*category: listening*

**34.** What is something they said this week that you have not acknowledged?  
*category: listening*

**35.** When do you find it hardest to really listen to them?  
*category: listening*

**36.** What would you want them to understand without you having to explain it?  
*category: needs*

**37.** What is a question you would like to ask them but have not?  
*category: curiosity*

**38.** Where do you soften a true feeling just to keep the peace?  
*category: avoidance*

**39.** What do you need to say out loud, even if nothing changes as a result?  
*category: honesty*

**40.** What is one thing you would like them to stop assuming about you?  
*category: identity*

**41.** What do you wish you had said in a recent argument?  
*category: conflict*

**42.** What is one small thing they could do that would make you feel heard?  
*category: needs*

## Arc 4 - Conflict and repair (days 43-56)

**43.** What is the argument the two of you keep having? What do you think it is really about?  
*category: perpetual*

**44.** When you are angry, do you move toward them or away? What would it take to not let the sun go down on it?  
*category: anger*

**45.** What is the first sign for you that a conversation is going badly?  
*category: escalation*

**46.** What do you need in the first minute of a disagreement?  
*category: needs*

**47.** What is something you have said in anger that you regret?  
*category: repair*

**48.** How do you know when you need to step away from a conversation?  
*category: self-awareness*

**49.** What would a good repair look like after a hard conversation?  
*category: repair*

**50.** What is a criticism you have made that was really about something else?  
*category: criticism*

**51.** Where do you find it hardest to let go of being right?  
*category: humility*

**52.** What is something you have never fully forgiven, even if you said you had?  
*category: forgiveness*

**53.** What do you do when you feel attacked?  
*category: defensiveness*

**54.** What is one thing you could yield on in the next disagreement — not because you were wrong, but to show you are for them?  
*category: mutual-yielding*

**55.** When did you last feel like one flesh during a disagreement?  
*category: unity*

**56.** What is one thing you want them to know about how you fight?  
*category: disclosure*

**63.** What is something you would like to do together that you never have?

**65.** What is one way they could make an ordinary evening better?  
*category: rituals*

*category: nostalgia*

**67.** What does a good evening look like to you?

**69.** When did you last feel chosen by them?

**87.** What are you grateful for that you have never told them?  
*category: gratitude*
